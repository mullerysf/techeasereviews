Amazon Echo Show 8 Review: The One Device I Recommend to Almost Every Senior

By Shawn · TechEase Reviews · 10 min read · Updated 2026
📋 Disclosure: TechEase Reviews participates in the Amazon Associates Program. If you click a link and make a purchase, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never influences my recommendations — I only recommend products I'd suggest to my own family.

I help seniors with technology every week in the Venice, Florida area. One question comes up constantly: "What's the one thing that would make my life easier?" My answer is almost always the Amazon Echo Show 8.

What Is the Echo Show 8, Exactly?

The Echo Show 8 is a smart display — think of it as a small touchscreen tablet that sits on your counter and responds to your voice. You speak to it, it answers. You can ask it the weather, set medication reminders, make video calls to family, watch YouTube, see your front doorbell camera, and control smart lights or your thermostat — all without touching a button.

For seniors who struggle with small phone screens or complicated apps, the Echo Show 8's 8-inch HD display is a game changer. The text is large, the interface is simple, and most importantly — it works without you having to remember how to use it.

What Seniors Love Most About It

The number one thing I hear from seniors after they've had the Echo Show 8 for a month: "I just talk to it." No unlocking a phone, no finding an app, no squinting at a screen. You say "Alexa, call my daughter" and it calls your daughter — with video, on the big screen.

Medication reminders are another huge hit. Set it up once and Alexa announces your medications at the same time every day. It doesn't forget. It doesn't need to be charged. It just works.

✓ Pros
  • Incredibly easy voice control — no buttons needed
  • Large, bright 8" display — easy to see
  • Video calls with family are simple and reliable
  • Medication & appointment reminders built in
  • Can display family photos as a slideshow
  • Smart home control (lights, thermostat, locks)

Is It Hard to Set Up?

Setup takes about 10 minutes and the Echo Show walks you through it on screen. You need your Wi-Fi password and an Amazon account (free to create). If that sounds daunting, it's a perfect excuse to call a local tech helper — or follow our setup guide here.

Once it's set up, it requires zero maintenance. It updates itself automatically, never needs to be charged (it plugs into the wall), and your family can call it from their phones using the free Alexa app.

Verdict: The Single Best Tech Purchase for Most Seniors

The Echo Show 8 earns its place on the counter of nearly everyone I recommend it to. It reduces phone anxiety, keeps families connected, and genuinely makes daily life easier. At $179.99, it's one of the best-value technology purchases available for older adults today.

Bottom Line

If you're buying one piece of technology for a senior parent, spouse, or neighbor — or for yourself — this is it. It doesn't require technical skill to use, it gets more useful over time, and it gives families peace of mind knowing their loved one has an easy way to connect and get reminders.
