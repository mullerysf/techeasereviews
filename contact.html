How Pricing Works

Every visit starts with a flat $95 house call fee — this covers your first hour of work no matter how fast the job goes. If a job runs longer, additional time is billed in 30-minute increments. Simple, fair, and easy to understand upfront. There are never surprise charges.

House Call / First Hour
$95
Covers every visit regardless of duration. Most jobs are done within this.
Additional Time
$45
Per additional half hour after the first hour.
Same-Day / Emergency
+$30
Added to standard rate for same-day availability.
💡
Example: A Nest thermostat install takes about 75 minutes → $95 + $45 = $140 total. You'll always be told the estimate before work begins.
